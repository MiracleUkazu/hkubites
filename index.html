Introducing HKU Bites

HKU Bites is a restaurant discovery and recommendation platform designed to make finding food around HKU easier. Whether you have a specific craving or dietary requirement, simply describe what you're looking for in everyday language and let HKU Bites find suitable recommendations based on your preferences, budget and location. Prefer browsing? Switch to Explore mode and use quick filters to discover your next favourite spot!
